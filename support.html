Subscription help

Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID. On iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then tap Subscriptions.

🔄

Restore a purchase

Use the Restore Purchases option inside Soravi. Confirm that the device is signed in with the Apple ID used for the original purchase.

Privacy requests

Email us with “Privacy Request” in the subject line for questions about access, correction, or deletion.

Frequently asked questions

Why did my packing list change?

Lists can vary based on the trip details, activities, preferences, or recommendations available when the list is generated.

Can I add or remove items?

Yes. Soravi is designed to let you personalize the recommended list for your own needs.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Apple manages billing. Open iPhone Settings, tap your name, choose Subscriptions, select Soravi, and follow Apple’s cancellation options.

How quickly will I receive a response?

We aim to respond to support requests within three business days. Response times may vary during launches and holidays.
