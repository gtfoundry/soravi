Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 5, 2026

GT Foundry (“Soravi,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) operates the Soravi mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

Depending on the features you use, Soravi may collect account information, trip details, packing-list content, customer-support communications, usage information, device information, diagnostics, and location information when you grant permission.

How we use information

We use information to generate packing lists, maintain accounts, sync trips, send reminders, provide support, improve the app, manage subscriptions, communicate service updates, and comply with legal obligations.

AI-generated content

Trip details submitted for list generation may be processed by an AI service provider solely to produce your packing list.

Payments and service providers

Subscription purchases may be processed by Apple and RevenueCat. We do not directly collect or store payment-card details.

Data retention and your choices

We retain account and trip data while your account is active and delete or anonymize personal information after an account-deletion request, subject to legal requirements. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, export, object to, or restrict processing of your information.

Children’s privacy

Soravi is not directed to children under 13 or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email soravi.app@gmail.com.
