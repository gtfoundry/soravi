Soravi Privacy Policy

GT Foundry ("Soravi," "we," "us," or "our") operates the Soravi mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

By using Soravi, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Account information: Email address, name (if provided), and authentication credentials when you sign up, including through Apple or Google sign-in.
  • Trip information: Destination, travel dates, trip type, planned activities, number of travelers, traveler age groups (if provided), and packing style you enter to generate a packing list.
  • Packing list content: Items, categories, quantities, and packed or unpacked status you create or edit.
  • Customer support communications: Information you provide when you contact us for support.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage data: How you interact with the App, such as features used, screens viewed, and session length, to help us improve the product.
  • Device information: Device type, operating system, and app version, used for compatibility and troubleshooting.
  • Diagnostics: Crash logs and performance data to identify and fix bugs.

1.3 Location Information

If you grant permission, we may use approximate or precise location to suggest your destination or provide weather-based packing recommendations. You can decline this at any time in your device settings; the App remains functional using manually entered destinations.

1.4 Information from Third Parties

  • Sign-in providers: If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive basic profile information, such as name and email, as permitted by your privacy settings with that provider.
  • Payment processors: Subscription purchases are processed by the Apple App Store, Google Play, or RevenueCat. We do not directly collect or store your payment-card details.
  • Weather data: If weather-based suggestions are enabled, we query third-party weather providers using your trip destination to return forecast information. We do not share your personal account details with these providers beyond the location query itself.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Generate personalized packing lists based on your trip details.
  • Maintain your account and sync your trips across devices.
  • Send trip-related notifications and reminders.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Improve and maintain the App, including diagnosing technical issues.
  • Process subscription purchases and manage Premium access.
  • Communicate service updates, security alerts, and administrative messages.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. AI-Generated Content

Soravi uses artificial intelligence to generate packing-list suggestions based on the trip details you provide. Trip details submitted for list generation may be processed by our AI service provider solely to produce your packing list and are not used to train third-party AI models on your personal data beyond what is necessary to deliver this feature.

4. Data Sharing

  • Service providers: Cloud hosting, analytics, push notifications, and payment processors that process data on our behalf under confidentiality obligations.
  • Collaborators: If you use Shared Trip Packing, trip and packing-list details you choose to share become visible to the travelers you invite.
  • Legal requirements: If required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request.
  • Business transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to standard confidentiality protections.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account and trip data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal information within 60 days, except where retention is required for legal, tax, or fraud-prevention purposes.

6. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your personal information; withdraw consent for optional features; or object to or restrict certain processing.

To exercise these rights, contact us at soravi.app@gmail.com. Residents of the EEA or UK may have rights under GDPR. California residents may have rights under the CCPA/CPRA, including the right to know, delete, and opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. Soravi does not sell personal information.

7. Children’s Privacy

Soravi is not directed to children under 13, or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. International Data Transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than your own. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards, such as standard contractual clauses, for such transfers.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes through the App or by email and update the “Last Updated” date.
